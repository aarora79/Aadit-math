Problem 6 · Area and probability

The dartboard split into four rectangles

The question A rectangular dartboard is subdivided into four smaller rectangles, as shown in the diagram, with two regions having areas 56 square units and 14 square units. Assume that when a dart is thrown it will hit the dartboard, and each point on the dartboard is equally likely to be hit. If the probability of getting a dart in the shaded region is 8/45, what is the probability of a dart hitting in the rectangle that has an area of 14 square units? Express your answer as a common fraction.
Hint 1
06Shared sides give you ratios

Two rectangles side by side have the same height. Their areas then compare the way their widths do. A probability here is one area divided by the whole area, which gives you an equation for the missing piece.

Give the two widths and two heights letters. The letters cancel when you divide, so you never need their real sizes.
Hint 2

The 56 and the 14 sit side by side, so the left column is 4 times as wide as the right one. The shaded piece is under the 56, so it fills 4/5 of the bottom row. Use 8/45 to work out what share of the whole board the bottom row is.

Step-by-step solution

Step by step

  1. Name the two widths and two heights

    One cut goes down, one goes across. That makes two column widths and two row heights.

    left width = L, right width = R top height = h, bottom height = k

  2. Compare the two top rectangles

    They sit in the same row, so they share the height h. Their areas then line up with their widths.

    56 / 14 = 4, so L = 4R

    Write R as w, which makes L equal to 4w.

    Every side is labeled. Each arrow shows the length it is next to. The left column is 4w wide and the right column is w wide. The top row is h tall and the bottom row is k tall. Each piece's area is its width times its height. Not to scale.

  3. Get the top row's area

    56 + 14 = 70 (4w + w) × h = 5wh = 70 wh = 14

  4. Write the shaded probability as areas

    The shaded piece is the bottom-left rectangle.

    shaded = 4w × k whole = 5w × (h + k) 4wk / [5w(h + k)] = 8/45

  5. Cancel w and solve for k

    The w appears on top and bottom, so it drops out.

    4k / [5(h + k)] = 8/45 180k = 40(h + k) 140k = 40h k = 2h / 7

  6. Find the whole board's area

    whole = 5w(h + k) = 5wh × (1 + 2/7) = 5 × 14 × 9/7 = 90

  7. Divide to get the probability

    14 / 90 = 7/45

  8. Check with the shaded piece

    shaded = 4wk = 4 × 14 × 2/7 = 16 16 / 90 = 8/45 ✓

    All four areas filled in. 56 + 14 + 16 + 4 = 90. Not to scale.

7/45

The move that makes this easy: the letters cancel. You never find out the real width or height of anything, and you do not need to. Whenever a figure is cut by straight lines, look for two pieces that share a side and read their areas as a ratio of the other side.
