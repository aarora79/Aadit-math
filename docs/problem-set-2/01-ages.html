Problem 1 · Ages

Aerith and Bob

The question Aerith is twice as old as Bob. Eight years ago, Aerith's age in years was equal to the square of Bob's age in years at that time. If Bob is more than 8 years old now, how many years old is Aerith now?
Hint 1
01One letter for every age

Pick one unknown, usually the younger person's age now. Write every other age with that same letter. “Eight years ago” means subtract 8 from both people.

BobAerith
nowb2b
8 years agob − 82b − 8
A square in the problem gives you a quadratic, so expect two answers. Use the extra clue to throw one away, then check which person the question asks about.
Hint 2

Let Bob be b now, so Aerith is 2b. Eight years ago they were b − 8 and 2b − 8. Write 2b − 8 = (b − 8)² and keep the answer that makes Bob older than 8.

Step-by-step solution

Step by step

  1. Name Bob's age now

    Call it b. Aerith is twice as old, so she is 2b. Eight years ago Bob was b − 8 and Aerith was 2b − 8.

  2. Turn the sentence into an equation

    “Aerith's age then equals the square of Bob's age then.”

    2b − 8 = (b − 8)²

  3. Multiply out and collect everything on one side

    (b − 8)² = b² − 16b + 64 2b − 8 = b² − 16b + 64 0 = b² − 18b + 72

  4. Factor

    Look for two numbers that multiply to 72 and add to 18. They are 6 and 12.

    (b − 6)(b − 12) = 0 → b = 6 or b = 12

  5. Use the extra clue

    Bob is more than 8 years old, so b = 6 is out. (With b = 6, Bob would have been −2 eight years ago.) Bob is 12.

  6. Answer the question that was asked

    The question wants Aerith, not Bob.

    Aerith = 2 × 12 = 24

  7. Check

    BobAerith
    now1224
    8 years ago416

    4² = 16, so it works. Watch out: 4 is Bob's age eight years ago. It is not the answer.

Aerith is 24 years old.

How to spot this type: two people's ages and a clue about some other time. Use one letter for one person's age now, build a small table of now and then, and write the clue as an equation. A square makes it a quadratic, so the problem always gives you a second clue to pick between the two roots.
