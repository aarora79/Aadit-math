02A diagonal cuts out special triangles

Add up the angles at each end of the diagonal. If a triangle's two known angles make 90°, its third angle is a right angle and the diagonal is the hypotenuse.

30-60-90 sides go 1 : √3 : 2. 45-45-90 sides go 1 : 1 : √2. Find each triangle's area on its own, then add.

03Price per piece is cost divided by count

Change dollars to cents first, then divide by the number of pieces each person paid for. Now the two prices are in the same unit and you can subtract.

Matthew16¢
Darren20¢
Ignore what nobody paid for. The 25 pieces Matthew ate do not change what each piece cost him.

04Area grows with the side squared

Shapes with the same form are similar. Double the side and the area goes up 4 times. So area, and a price based on area, is a fixed number times side².

Similar shapes on the three sides of a right triangle obey Pythagoras too: the two small ones add up to the big one. Use the prices as areas.
