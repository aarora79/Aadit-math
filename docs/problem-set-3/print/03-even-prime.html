Problem 3 · Easy · The even prime

Two primes that add to 85

The question The sum of two prime numbers is 85. What is the product of these two primes?
032 is the only even prime

Every prime except 2 is odd. Odd + odd is always even. So if two primes add up to an odd number, one of them has to be 2.

When two primes add to an odd number, subtract 2 and check that what is left is prime.

Step by step

  1. Look at odd and even

    Two odd numbers always add to an even number. For example 3 + 5 = 8.

  2. One prime must be 2

    85 is odd, so the two primes cannot both be odd. One of them is even. The only even prime is 2.

  3. Find the other prime

    85 − 2 = 83

  4. Check that 83 is prime

    Test the primes up to 7, because 11 × 11 = 121 is bigger than 83. 83 is odd. Its digits add to 11, so 3 does not go in. It does not end in 0 or 5. 7 × 11 = 77 and 7 × 12 = 84, so 7 does not go in. 83 is prime.

  5. Multiply

    2 × 83 = 166
The product is 166.

How to spot this type: two primes with an odd sum (or an odd difference). One of them must be 2. The question asks for the product, so do not stop at 83.
