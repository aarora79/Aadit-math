All the factors of 360

The question How many positive divisors does 360 have?
04Add one to each power, then multiply

Write the number as primes with powers. A divisor chooses how many of each prime to use, from zero up to the power. Multiply the number of choices together.

12 = 2² × 3¹ has (2 + 1) × (1 + 1) = 6 divisors: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12.

Step by step

  1. Break 360 into primes

    360 = 2 × 180 = 2 × 2 × 90 = 2 × 2 × 2 × 45 = 2 × 2 × 2 × 3 × 3 × 5 = 2³ × 3² × 5¹

  2. Count the choices for each prime

    A divisor can use the prime 2 zero, one, two or three times. The same idea works for 3 and 5.

    power of 2: 0, 1, 2, 3 → 4 choices power of 3: 0, 1, 2 → 3 choices power of 5: 0, 1 → 2 choices

  3. Multiply the choices

    Each mix of choices gives a different divisor.

    4 × 3 × 2 = 24

  4. Check by pairing

    Divisors come in pairs that multiply to 360. There are 12 pairs, so 24 divisors.

    1×360 2×180 3×120 4×90 5×72 6×60 8×45 9×40 10×36 12×30 15×24 18×20
360 has 24 positive divisors.

How to spot this type: “how many divisors” or “how many factors”. Factor into primes, add 1 to each power, multiply. If you forget the zero choice you get 3 × 2 × 1 = 6, which is far too small.
