Six concept cards

Print on A4, then cut along the card edges. One card per problem.

01A ratio is equal groups

“3 out of every 5” breaks the whole into groups of 5. Each group hands 3 to one side and 2 to the other. Count the groups first.

The parts add back to the whole: 3 + 2 = 5. Read the question again to see which part it wants.
02Remainders run in cycles

A remainder counts up, then starts over. By 3 it starts over every 3 numbers. By 4 it takes 4. They line up again every 12 numbers, the least common multiple of 3 and 4.

03Same distance, two rates

Both runners cover the same distance. Call the slower time t, so the faster time is t minus the head start you were given. Speed is distance divided by time, and that turns the speed gap into an equation.

04Mean and median say different things

Put the values in order, smallest to largest. The mean gives you the total of all four. From the median you get the sum of the middle two. You now have two equations.

With one more value given, subtraction finds the last one. Check the answer: the smallest value cannot be bigger than the mean.
05Two staircases make a rectangle

Stack 1 + 2 + 3 and so on as a staircase. Turn a second copy upside down and drop it on top. The two fill a solid rectangle n wide and n+1 tall, so one staircase is half of it.

rectangle = n(n+1)

To find the biggest n under a limit, start near the square root of twice that limit. Test that n and the one after it. The question asks for n, not for the sum.
