Problem 4 · Mean and median

Evan's four test scores

The question Evan takes four math tests. The median of his four test scores is 81 and the mean is 75. If the highest of his four test scores is 97, what is the lowest of his scores?
04Mean and median say different things

Put the values in order, smallest to largest. The mean gives you the total of all four. From the median you get the sum of the middle two. You now have two equations.

abcd
With one more value given, subtraction finds the last one. Check the answer: the smallest value cannot be bigger than the mean.

Step by step

  1. Put the four scores in order and name them

    a ≤ b ≤ c ≤ d

  2. Turn the mean into a total

    The mean is the total divided by how many there are, so multiply back.

    a + b + c + d = 75 × 4 = 300

  3. Turn the median into the middle pair

    With an even count, the median is the average of the two middle scores.

    (b + c) ÷ 2 = 81 b + c = 162

  4. Use the highest score

    The largest of the four is 97, so d = 97.

  5. Subtract to find the lowest

    a = 300 − 97 − 162 a = 41

  6. Check that the order still works

    Any b and c that add to 162 will do, say 80 and 82.

    41, 80, 82, 97 total = 300 → mean 75 ✓ middle = (80 + 82) ÷ 2 = 81 ✓ highest = 97 ✓
Evan's lowest score is 41.
